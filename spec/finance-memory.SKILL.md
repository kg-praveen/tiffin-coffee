## PART A — THE ELEVEN PROMOTED PRINCIPLES
Harvested from 42 transcribed Money Pechu episodes. Each crossed the ~5/42 repetition
threshold, which is the conviction signal (same logic as source-count in the 164-name
list). **These fire on every investment decision; they do not need to be looked up.**

**1. GOLD IS THE HEDGE LEG, NOT A SECTOR (23/42 — the dominant theme).**
The pattaz list is a **deliberately hedged structure**, not a list of picks. *"You have
GOLD — that's why you can look at Natco falling 30% and still see a way through."*
Evidence, from his own statement of position: through a real drawdown his equity book
was **down ~9.5% against a Nifty −15%**, and the gold leg turned the household total
positive. **Never read household gold as an over-concentration to be managed down.**

**2. TIFFIN-COFFEE — SMALL CONTINUOUS BUYING (18/42).**
*"Buying at higher levels and lower levels, your AVERAGE will be much BELOW the market
break-even."* Brokerage is proportional (SEBI 2.5% cap) so small tickets cost nothing
extra. The habit is the edge; the timing is not.

**3. PROMOTER / OWNER INTEGRITY IS THE GATE (15/42).**
*"A wonderful company can DEGRADE into an ordinary one. The variable is the owner."*
**The Golden Tobacco rule: a moat protects the BUSINESS; the OWNER decides whether
value reaches the SHAREHOLDER.** Score the owner before the moat.

**4. THE CAPITAL STAYS SAFE; ONLY THE YIELD GOES TO EQUITY (13/42).**
***"The big-level money ALWAYS stays in the BANK. Buy equity only with the INTEREST.
Your PRINCIPAL is protected."*** Lumpsum → bonds/FD → interest → monthly equity →
reinvest dividends into the same name. **He states this as a prohibition, not a
preference.** ⚠️ It challenges the ULIP ₹18.9L plan — see `household-finance` §8.

**5. NEVER EMOTIONALLY ATTACH (12/42).**
Money is stored labour, not identity. Invest only money not needed for 10 years (5 at
best). **If a position hurts enough to watch daily, IT IS TOO BIG.** Panic is a SIZING
problem, not a character problem.

**6. SPEND LESS THAN YOU EARN (10/42).**
*"If income is ₹1,000, spend ₹900. That is the ONLY mantra."* 10% floor, 20-30%
target, 50% is Templeton's ceiling. **Never let the number drift toward zero.**

**7. BOOK VALUE, NOT P/E, FOR BANKS AND NBFCs (9/42).**
His stated ceiling: ideal ~1.3× book — **[DOCTRINE] his PREFERENCE, used at P5
context only. The household's LIVE gate is the ROE-linked justified P/B in osep v7
§G-LENDER (adopted 05-Sep-2026). Do not apply 1.3x as our gate — defect-1.** Method: **ten years of book value → plot the growth →
project five years forward.** The NBFC proof: Muthoot P/E 15 / P/B 3.19× vs Manappuram
P/E 16 / P/B 1.3-1.4× → he takes Manappuram. **The P/E ordering inverts the P/B
ordering; go with book.**

**8. EARNINGS DECIDE THE MARKET (9/42).**
*"It is EARNINGS. Nothing else."* The test: are earnings growing at 15%?
Index anchors [DOCTRINE, market-wide — he never varies the anchor by sector]:
long-run 17-18x, mid-point ~19-20x, downside 15-16x.
⚠️ One transcription conflict on whether markets *can* or *cannot* stay detached from
reality for long. **The reading that fits his own argument: irrational in the SHORT
run, earnings-anchored in the LONG run.** Flagged, not closed.

**9. DON'T TRADE — TIME IN THE MARKET (8/42).**
***"Ten years' worth of gain comes in about FIFTY TRADING DAYS."*** Miss them and the
decade is gone. On swing trading: *"Don't. One day you WILL get hit."*
Counterweight, held together: **the three lost decades** — Japan ~30 years, Dow
1929-54, Nasdaq 2000-21. **Stay invested, but never ONLY invested.**

**10. OWN THE LENDER, DON'T BE THE BORROWER (7/42).**
*"Whichever bank you borrow from — PAY ME THE INTEREST. I hold shares in every bank."*
Banking = leverage × spread: ₹10 equity → ₹300 deposits → lend at a spread.

**11. CIRCLE OF COMPETENCE — AS VERIFIABILITY (5/42).**
Not "do I understand it" (an infinite excuse) but **"what public document proves the
thesis variable?"** See the CIRCLE MAP below for how to weight HIS view by domain.
